What to look at rev 2

Focus your review on (rev 2 changes):
  • Motif→character transition — does the crossfade from the scenery-motif wordmark to the typographic "LitCrop" read as a "logo reveal"? Press ↻ replay in the hero top-right to re-watch.
  • Revised motifs: does t = hoe read as a tool? Does C = sun look like a sun arc vs. a crop leaf? Does r = seedling sprout correctly?
  • "o" proposal — I chose a round ripe fruit with stem (terracotta, tomato-like). Alternatives: moon, water basin, flower bloom, harvest basket. Tell me if this isn't right.
  • p = bowing wheat — does the bowl droop in a way that reads as "wheat bent under its own weight"? This is the hardest glyph because a normal lowercase p bowl goes up, not down.
  • Simplified waterfall — reduced from 3 water curves + 2 dashed overlays to 1 solid + 1 dashed. Is it still recognisable as a waterfall?
  • Mobile = scaled desktop — same two trees + farmhouse + scenery, just in the phone column. Confirm the scaling works.
  • Typographic wordmark style — the final "LitCrop" uses system sans-serif (Inter / Helvetica) bold. Would a serif (Georgia) or slab serif feel more "earthy"?
  • Reduced-motion fallback — toggle macOS "Reduce motion" and reload. Final state should show the typographic wordmark, not the motifs.

What's NOT done yet

  • These are PoC artwork — the SVG paths are sketched, not refined. Final art in Step 8 will tighten curves, align letter baselines, and polish spacing.
  • The form fields are decorative — they're not wired to Cognito.
  • i18n (JA) not shown in the mockup, but the real implementation inherits the existing i18n keys.
  • No brand asset export yet (Production scope) — the SVG is still inline, not saved as litcrop-wordmark.svg.
