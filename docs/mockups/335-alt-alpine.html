Alpine Exploration — what this mockup is testing

This is an exploratory alternate direction for #335, informed by docs/screenshots/IMG_1193.jpeg and docs/screenshots/IMG_1102.jpeg (Japanese alpine winter dawn scenery) and cross-referenced with .agent/prompts/design/litcrop_design_prompt_full.md. It is not committed and is presented to show what the design could look like if we pivoted from the current rolling-hills composition to a more dramatic alpine setting.

← 335-desktop.html (committed rev 9) 335-mobile.html (committed rev 9) →

Key differences from the committed rev 9

  • Panoramic landscape viewBox (1200 × 700) instead of portrait square (720 × 720). Captures the natural horizontal sweep of an alpine scene.
  • Pastel sky gradient — warm peach at the top fading to pale gold → earthy cream. Evokes the golden-hour light in IMG_1102.
  • Dramatic snow-capped peaks — 2 main jagged apexes with white snow-cap tracing and drift-tick texture on the upper slopes. Much more vertical energy than the gentle 3-layer ridges of rev 9.
  • Evergreen foreground silhouettes — 6 pine/cedar trees in dark forest green, with subtle sway animation. Replaces the 2 sketched trees of rev 9.
  • Wooden cabin with pitched roof + chimney smoke — more detailed than the rev 9 farmhouse, positioned mid-ground.
  • Bare branch frame in the top-left corner with dried-leaf dots — an optional decorative framing element from IMG_1193.
  • Valley mist — 2 horizontal flowing lines between the mountains to suggest atmospheric haze.
  • Extended palette — adds 4 alpine-specific colors (--alpine-sky-*, --alpine-snow, --alpine-peak, --alpine-evergreen) layered on top of the earthy base.

What stays the same as rev 9

  • 7 letter motifs (L waterfall, i person, t hoe, C sun, r seedling, o water drop, p bowing wheat) — reused verbatim, rescaled for landscape
  • Final brand lockup (crop sprout icon + Inter 800 "LitCrop" typographic wordmark) — unchanged
  • Tagline "Little, Light and Lit, then Enlight" in Brush Script MT cursive — unchanged
  • Animation timeline structure (scenery → motifs → final lockup with scenery dim) — unchanged
  • Reduced-motion fallback — behaviorally identical

Trade-offs to consider

  • Mobile adaptation is harder — landscape 1200×700 doesn't collapse neatly into the 480px phone column. Would need either (a) a portrait crop losing the panoramic feel, or (b) scaling down with letterboxing, or (c) a separately-authored mobile SVG. More implementation work.
  • More complex SVG — 6 trees, cabin detail, snow texture ticks, mist lines, 2 mountain ridges → ~50% more paths than rev 9. Slight perf impact (still <15 KB gzipped).
  • New color tokens needed — 4 alpine-specific tokens would need to go into tokens.css if adopted. Retires the "no new tokens" MVP assumption from DESIGNS-335.
  • More photographic vs minimalist — this direction is richer/more representational; rev 9 is sparser/more abstract. Preference depends on which vibe you want for the brand.
  • Reset to Step 3 — adopting this means re-entering the pipeline at Step 3 (UX/UI), which invalidates DESIGNS-335 + TASK-BREAKDOWN-335 + PLANS-335 and requires regeneration.

How to decide

Open this file and the committed rev 9 mockups side-by-side in your browser. Compare the first-impression feel: does rev 9 feel too sparse/sketchy? Does this alpine version feel too busy/illustrated? The right call depends on which mood fits LitCrop's brand voice — minimal/calm farmer-tool vs. atmospheric/poetic farming-lifestyle.

If you like this direction, next step is to re-enter the pipeline at Step 3 (UX/UI design) and promote the alpine composition to a new committed mockup revision. If you prefer rev 9, this file can be deleted (it's uncommitted).
