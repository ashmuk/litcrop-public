Design Notes
Privacy

Geo Location — Optional for Farm Creation (#277)

Current: latitude/longitude are mandatory. Change: city/region is the minimum requirement. Map picker remains but is skippable. Coordinates opt-in for elevation data and future AI weather context. Existing farms with coordinates are unaffected.
